# Code of Conduct

## Expected behavior

Contributors are expected to be respectful, direct, and constructive. Keep
discussions focused on the project and assume good intent while resolving
disagreements.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and
publication of private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns through the repository's private reporting tools when
available. Include enough context for the maintainers to understand what
happened. Reports will be reviewed and handled as privately as possible.
